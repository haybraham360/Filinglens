From EDGAR to a cited answer

  1. Ingest

    Filings and XBRL facts come straight from SEC EDGAR, rate-limited, cached, and keyed on CIK so ticker changes never break history.

  2. Parse

    Each filing is split into its Items (Business, Risk Factors, MD&A, Market Risk) and chunked without crossing section boundaries.

  3. Retrieve

    Hybrid search combines meaning and exact terms, filtered to the right company and year, then reranked for precision.

  4. Answer

    The model answers only from retrieved text. Every quote is checked against its source before you see it.

The model explains. Code does the math.

Language models are unreliable at arithmetic, so FilingLens never lets one produce a reported figure. An agent calls deterministic tools for financials and ratios, shows its formula and inputs, and only then writes the explanation.

Tools: search_filings, compare_sections, get_financials, compute_ratio. Six steps at most per question.

Every change has to beat the last score

A hand-verified golden set of questions, a slice of FinanceBench, and a smoke test on every pull request. Chunk size, hybrid search, reranking and prompts are kept only when the numbers improve. These are the bars each release must clear.

≥ 95%of citation quotes found verbatim in the cited chunk
≥ 90%of agent questions call the expected tools
0numeric answers produced without a tool call
< 8 sp95 latency for an answer without reranking

Unanswerable questions are part of the test set. Saying “the filings don’t say” counts as a correct answer.

Do quiet rewrites predict returns?

Cohen, Malloy and Nguyen (Lazy Prices, Journal of Finance, 2020) found that firms which change their annual report language tend to underperform, and that the market takes months to notice. FilingLens replicates their four similarity measures and tests whether embedding-based signals add anything, including on filings after 2014, which the paper never saw.

A portfolio long non-changers and short changers earned up to 188 basis points a month in the paper’s strongest specification, with returns building for up to 18 months and no reversal.

  1. Score each 10-K against the last one

    Classical and embedding similarity, new and removed risk headings, and the change in negative-word share. Timestamped at SEC acceptance.

  2. Sort into quintiles

    Breakpoints come from the prior year only. A stock enters its portfolio the month after its filing.

  3. Hold and rebalance

    3, 6 and 12 month holding periods with overlapping cohorts, equal and value weighted.

  4. Test the alpha

    Fama–French 3 and 5 factor plus momentum regressions with Newey–West errors. Every variant tried is reported, not only the best.
